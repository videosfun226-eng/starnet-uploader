Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool. This policy explains what it handles.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar), to confirm which account is signed in.
  • An access token and refresh token, so it can post on your behalf.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local folder that is not synced to cloud storage. The app has no server and does not send your data anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or rent any data.
  • It does not read your followers, messages, or other users' data.
  • It does not use cookies or analytics.

Deleting your data

Revoke the app's access in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local sign-in folder removes all stored tokens.
